# Marqo

**Brand, web, story.**

A small studio in Porto helping ambitious teams ship work that feels like
theirs. Identity, websites, narrative - end to end.

## What we do

- **Brand identity** - naming, logo, type, palette, guidelines. The visual
  language your team can run with.
- **Web & product** - marketing sites, landing pages, and product UI. Built
  fast, fast to load, easy to maintain.
- **Story & content** - positioning, voice, copy, case studies. We help you
  say something worth saying.

## Studio

Five people in Porto. Two designers, a product manager, an engineer, and the
founder. We work directly with the people we help, and we keep the team small
on purpose.

- Founded 2026
- Based in Porto, Portugal
- Working in English and Portuguese
